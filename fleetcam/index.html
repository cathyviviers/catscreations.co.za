FAQ

Questions fleet managers ask us

What is vehicle video telematics?

It combines GPS and vehicle data with live and recorded video, sent over the cellular network. You see where a vehicle is, how it's being driven and what's happening around it, all in one place.

Can I watch my vehicles live?

Yes. Live and recorded video is available from any connected device through the cloud-based portal, and critical events upload automatically.

Are FleetCam products ICASA approved?

Yes. Every FleetCam product carries ICASA approval.

Will cameras lower my insurance?

Many insurers offer discounts for fleets that use video telematics, depending on your current policy. We're happy to provide the information your broker needs.

Do you work with small fleets?

Absolutely. FleetCam scales from a single vehicle to thousands, on the same platform and with the same 24/7 Bureau behind it.

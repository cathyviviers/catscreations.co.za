About Me

Hello, I'm Cat

I'm a brand identity designer based in Pretoria, South Africa, with nineteen years in design, print and marketing. I started out running my own print shop, so I know what happens to a design after you hand it over. Alongside my team I build logos, brand systems, and everything that carries them: packaging, print, signage and social.

Nineteen years, starting with my own print shop. I learned bleed, trim, stock weight and exactly what a printer will reject before I was ever designing files for someone else to print.

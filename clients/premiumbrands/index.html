    About us

    Our journey started in 1999. Today we sit at the heart of the Premium Group, representing some of the world's premier professional and consumer technology brands across Africa.

    What sets us apart is simple: we actively build every brand we carry, and we make sure our service centre can support customers nationally. Specialist expertise, a proven history, and a team that cares what happens after the sale.

    1. 1999

      The journey starts

      Premium Brand Distributors opens its doors, focused on photographic and electronic brands.

    2. Imaging

      Nikon & Sony

      Authorised distributor and after-sales service provider for Nikon photographic and Sport Optics, and Sony photographic.

    3. Creators

      Storage, video & live

      Lexar, Angelbird, Atomos and YoloLiv join the portfolio for photographers, filmmakers and streamers.

    4. Smart living

      Anker Innovations

      Appointed to grow eufy Security, eufy Appliances and Anker PowerHouse in South Africa.

    5. Today

      Premium House, Centurion

      Head office, central warehouse and authorised service centre, all at Grey Owl Village.

The starting point

Today vs. the new Premium Brand

You've taken brands from under 8% share to market leaders. The current site doesn't tell that story yet, and a few small details quietly undersell a premium business.

Today

  • An old copyright line. The footer still says © 2019, which makes the whole site feel older than it is.
  • Two founding years. About says the journey started in 2009, while Distribution Services says 1999.
  • The same news summary on every post. Home page stories all share one excerpt about a zoom lens.
  • A 550-row dealer table. One long list, with internal notes such as "order nos only" and "new owners" showing publicly.
  • Repairs in pieces. Process, fees, drop-offs and terms are spread across five separate pages.
  • Survey slips. The quality survey asks one question twice and ends with a typo.

The new site

  • Always current. A fresh design, a living news feed and a footer that stays up to date on its own.
  • One clear story. A single company timeline, from 1999 to Premium House today.
  • Real headlines, real photos. Each story gets its own image and one-line summary.
  • A real dealer finder. Search 546 cleaned-up dealers by town or store, filter by province and tap to call.
  • One repair journey. Fees, timings, FAQs and drop-off points together, plus a repair tracker.
  • Proofed and polished. Every form rewritten, proofread and routed straight to the right team.

About this preview

What's real and what's simulated

Real

The brands, logos, product photos, news stories, team, company story and contact details come from your current site and materials. The dealer finder uses your own list of 546 dealers, cleaned up.

Simulated

The repair tracker accepts any job number and the forms don't send anything. Nothing is saved, and no customer data is collected.

To confirm

Some copy was written by us to show the idea, such as the partner pitch. Every word needs your sign-off, and each brand's own guidelines apply.

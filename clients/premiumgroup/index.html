        Good to know

        Do I need to service my camera?

        DSLR cameras have moving parts that may need servicing from time to time. Mirrorless cameras have no moving shutter mirror, so they need servicing less often.

        Can I clean my image sensor myself?

        Preferably not. Cleaning it the wrong way risks scratching the sensor, a battery failure mid-clean, or damage to the shutter and mirror assembly. Dust shows up as slightly blurry spots in your images.

        What does a sensor clean cost?

        Your first sensor clean after purchase is free. Every clean after that is R57.50.

        How should I pack gear for the courier?

        Use a sturdy container and bubble wrap, and never attach a lens to a camera. For warranty repairs, send the item in its original box with everything that came in it, plus your proof of purchase and the completed repair request form.

        What if I don't accept the quote?

        The inspection fee covers admin and labour. Uncollected items are archived after 6 weeks, with an R180 fee to retrieve them.

        What does "end of service period" mean?

        Once a model's service period ends, the manufacturer no longer makes the parts needed to repair it.

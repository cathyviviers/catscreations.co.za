The big idea

Free class in.
Paid workshops out.

LightCraft becomes the front door. Every free booking ends with a recommended paid workshop, from a creator who shoots the photos that person wants to take.

How it works

One path from new owner to paying student

01 · ATTRACT

New Nikon owner

Finds Nikon School through an ad, a creator's post, an in-box card or the retailer.

02 · START FREE

Books LightCraft

Two-minute booking, camera model captured, reminders sent automatically.

03 · RECOMMEND

Gets a next step

The confirmation, the class and the follow-up all point to a matching paid workshop.

04 · CONVERT

Books a paid workshop

Pays by card, instant EFT or QR. The dashboard tracks free-to-paid conversion.

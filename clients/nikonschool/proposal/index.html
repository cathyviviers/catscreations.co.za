The starting point

Today vs. the new Nikon School

The current site works, technically. But it was set up as an online shop, so workshops are sold like boxes on a shelf. People booking a class need different information.

Today

  • Shop language everywhere: "Regular price, Sale price", "Your cart is empty", "new collections and exclusive offers".
  • Listings show a title and a price, with no date, city, host or seats left. Visitors click into every product to find out when it is.
  • The free LightCraft class sits next to the paid ones with nothing linking them, so it doesn't lead anyone to a paid workshop.
  • The News page has no clear job, and ambassadors and hosts have nowhere to live on the site.
  • There's no simple way to see which ad, creator or campaign brought in a booking.
  • A stock theme with generic fonts and "Powered by Shopify" in the footer. It doesn't feel like Nikon.

The new site

  • Written and built for workshops: dates, venues, hosts, what you'll learn, what to bring.
  • Every card shows the next date, the city and live seats left. "4 seats left" gets people to book.
  • A clear learning path. LightCraft is the front door, and every free booking ends with a recommended paid workshop.
  • News becomes Ambassadors & Creators: one page per person, each with its own link to use in ads.
  • Bookings that come through a creator's link are credited to them, so you can see which partnerships sell seats.
  • Unmistakably Nikon: black, yellow, bold type and big imagery, built mobile-first.
The big idea

Free class in, paid workshops out

The free fundamentals workshop is the best marketing Nikon School has. The new site treats it as the first step of a path rather than one product among many.

01 · ATTRACTNew Nikon ownerFinds Nikon School through an ad, a creator's post, an in-box card or the retailer.
02 · START FREEBooks LightCraftTwo-minute booking, camera model captured, reminders sent automatically.
03 · RECOMMENDGets a next stepThe confirmation, the class and the follow-up email all point to a matching paid workshop.
04 · CONVERTBooks a paid workshopPays by card, instant EFT or QR. The dashboard tracks free-to-paid conversion.

Behind the scenes

How it plugs together

This preview simulates the moving parts. In the live build each one connects to a real service, chosen with Nikon's team.

PaymentsPayFast, Yoco or Peach Payments for card, instant EFT and QR. If Nikon would rather keep Shopify, it can stay behind the scenes as the checkout.
Content managementA simple dashboard for workshops, dates, people and photos. Nikon's marketing team runs it day to day.
EmailAutomatic confirmations, calendar invites, reminders the day before, and a follow-up with the next recommended workshop.
TrackingGoogle Analytics and Meta pixel, plus tagged links per creator and campaign, so every rand of ad spend can be traced to bookings.
Getting there

Suggested phases

Indicative only. We'll firm this up together after a discovery session.

Phase 1

Discovery and content

Agree the workshop list, people, payment gateway and who manages what. Gather photography, bios and brand assets from Nikon SA.

Phase 2

Design sign-off

Refine this concept with real imagery and copy, and sign off the home, workshop, booking and creator pages.

Phase 3

Build and integrations

Connect the payment gateway, dashboard, email and analytics. Move existing workshops and set up redirects from the old URLs.

Phase 4

Testing and training

Test real bookings end to end on phones and desktops, then a hands-on session so the team can run the dashboard with confidence.

Phase 5

Launch and support

Go live on nikonschool.co.za, keep an eye on bookings and tracking, and tweak based on what the numbers say.

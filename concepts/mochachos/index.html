Our story

Three cultures.
One open flame.

Mochachos brings together the zest of Mexican street food, the depth of Portuguese grilling and the warmth of a South African braai. The result is food that's loud, generous and made to share.

Every chicken is butterfly-cut and grilled over open flame for an even char, juicy meat and less fat. No shortcuts.

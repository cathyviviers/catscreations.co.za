5 Signs Your Brand Needs a Refresh

August 2026 · 4 min read

Written by

Sabine

Your logo looked great five years ago. Your colours felt fresh, your fonts felt modern, and everything was on-brand. But time moves fast — and so does design. Whether you're a small business in Pretoria or building your brand online across South Africa, here are five signs it might be time to give your brand a glow-up.

1. Your logo looks pixelated or outdated

If your logo was designed before the era of high-resolution screens, there's a good chance it looks blurry on modern devices. A well-built logo should exist as a vector file — scalable to any size without losing sharpness. If yours doesn't, that's a red flag.

2. Your brand no longer reflects what you do

Businesses evolve. If you've added services, changed your target market, or shifted your tone — but your branding is still frozen in time — there's a disconnect. Your visuals should tell the same story your business is telling today.

3. You're embarrassed to hand out your business card

That gut feeling? Trust it. If you hesitate before sharing your logo or website, it's worth asking why. Your brand should make you feel proud and confident, not apologetic.

4. Your competitors look more polished

You don't need to copy anyone — but if you notice that competitors are consistently looking more professional and cohesive, it might be time to level up. First impressions count, and design is often what tips the decision in your favour.

5. Nothing is consistent

Your Instagram uses different colours than your website. Your proposals have a different font than your email signature. Inconsistency erodes trust — even when the individual pieces look decent on their own. A brand refresh isn't just about a new logo; it's about building a system that works together.

Client Love & Why I Do This

August 2026 · 4 min read

Written by

Cathy Viviers

I've been sitting with a lot of gratitude lately. Not the kind you feel on a good day and forget by evening — the deep, settled kind that comes when you realise you are exactly where you're supposed to be, doing exactly what you love.

Recently, I took the time to go through the feedback and reviews that clients have left for me over the years. And honestly? I had to stop and just breathe for a moment. Reading words like "she was able to present my thoughts and vision visually" and "she went above and beyond my expectations" — those are not small things. Those are the words that remind me why I started Cat's Creations in the first place.

Client satisfaction is the only metric that matters to me

I'm a numbers person in some ways — I care about deadlines, file specifications, print margins, pixel dimensions. But when it comes to measuring the success of a project, there is only one question I ask myself: does this client feel seen?

Not just pleased. Not just "fine with it." But genuinely seen — like the design in front of them truly captures who they are and what they're trying to say to the world.

That feeling, when a client comes back and says "this is exactly it" — I don't think I'll ever get tired of it. It's better than any award. It's the reason I pour so much care into every brief, every revision, every late-night tweak before a deadline.

To every client who has taken the time to leave a kind word, recommend me to someone they know, or simply come back for another project — thank you. From the bottom of my heart. You are the reason Cat's Creations exists, and the reason it keeps growing.

Working with me through Upwork — safe, simple, and protected

One of the platforms where I've built some of my most rewarding client relationships is Upwork. If you've never used it before, here's why it's worth knowing about.

Upwork is a global freelancing platform that connects businesses with professional freelancers — and it comes with built-in protections that make hiring a designer feel safe and straightforward, whether you're in Pretoria or anywhere else in the world.

Why Upwork works so well for clients

  • ✓ Payment protection — your money is held securely in escrow and only released when you're satisfied with the work. You're never paying blindly.
  • ✓ Verified reviews — every rating on my profile comes from a completed, paid project. No fake reviews, no inflated stars.
  • ✓ Clear communication — all project messages, files, and milestones are tracked in one place. Nothing gets lost in an email thread.
  • ✓ Dispute resolution — if anything ever goes wrong (it hasn't, but just in case), Upwork has a formal process to protect both parties fairly.
  • ✓ My track record is visible — you can read every review, see the types of projects I've completed, and make an informed decision before we even say hello.

I've completed over 32 projects through Upwork with a 5.0 rating — and I'm proud of every single one. The platform has allowed me to work with clients across South Africa and internationally, all while maintaining the same standard of care I bring to every local Pretoria project.

You don't have to choose between local and online

One of the things I love most about how Cat's Creations has grown is that geography is no longer a barrier. If you're a small business owner in Pretoria, I can meet you for a coffee and talk through your brief in person. If you're a brand manager in Cape Town or a startup founder in London, we can kick off your project on Upwork today — with the same quality, the same responsiveness, and the same commitment to getting it right.

The tools have changed. The heart behind the work hasn't.
